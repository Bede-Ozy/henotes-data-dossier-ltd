AI Tools for Business

AI tools are transforming how businesses operate across nearly every function, helping teams work faster, make better decisions, and focus more time on high-value work. From content creation and design to data analysis, customer support, and software development, these tools are reshaping everyday workflows. This document categorizes AI tools by business function to help organizations identify the right solutions for their needs.

Category AI Tools What They Help With
Data Analysis & Business Intelligence Claude, ChatGPT, Julius AI, Power BI Copilot, Excel Copilot Analyze spreadsheets, identify trends, generate dashboards, summarize reports, and answer business questions using natural language.
Meeting & Note Taking Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, Granola Record meetings, transcribe conversations, summarize discussions, and generate action items automatically.
Customer Support Intercom Fin, Zendesk AI, Freshdesk AI Build AI-powered customer support agents that answer common questions and improve response times.
